3. Earn the right to scale

One primary conversion metric. Independent, randomized users. Predeclared fixed window. Default: 5/100 control versus 10/100 treatment.

Approximate 95% Newcombe interval from Wilson bounds. This is not a sequential test. Repeated peeking, non-random allocation, correlated accounts, multiple comparisons and late conversions can invalidate the interpretation. A conversion lift does not establish profitable incrementality.

4. Take a decision to the room

Transparent assumptions

Expected wins = spend ÷ CPC × lead rate × qualification rate × win rate. Horizon revenue = expected wins × annual contract value × months ÷ 12. Contribution = revenue × gross margin − acquisition spend. CAC = spend ÷ wins; payback = CAC ÷ monthly gross profit per customer.

No churn, cohort ramp, attribution overlap, saturation, fixed costs, sales costs, tax or revenue lag. Fractional wins are expectations. All customers are assumed active from the beginning of the horizon. Cash collection can differ from recognized revenue.

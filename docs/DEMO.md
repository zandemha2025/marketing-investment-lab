# Two-minute demo

- **0:00–0:20:** “This is a synthetic marketing budget worksheet. The decision is whether to fund this channel mix—not whether a dashboard looks attractive.” Show the budget, reserve and explicit ceilings.
- **0:20–0:45:** Change budget from $30,000 to $10,000. Show deployed spend falling to $9,000 and $1,000 held back. Explain that ceilings do not silently force redistribution.
- **0:45–1:05:** Restore budget and reduce gross margin to 20%. Inspect contribution and the negative-channel warning. “The same acquisition rate can become a bad investment when unit economics change.” Compare the downside case.
- **1:05–1:35:** Show 5/100 versus 10/100. “Doubling the observed conversion rate is not enough; the interval crosses zero and the verdict is inconclusive.” Uncheck the completed window to show the wait gate. Mention randomization, one primary metric and guardrails.
- **1:35–2:00:** Reset economics, complete the window and export the memo. Show assumptions, exact inputs, sensitivity cases and observations. “I would take this to finance and sales as a decision hypothesis; these are not measured customer results.”
